# Our Services page: content enhancement report

Page: `/our-services` (`src/app/our-services/page.tsx`, `src/config/site.ts`, `src/content/why.ts`)
References reviewed:
- The live Namokar page `namokareyecare.com/our-services`. It is the currently published version of this same site, so its text matched our page and gave nothing new to compare.
- The Pixel Eye Hospital all-services page (`/service`), used only for structure and tone. It opens with a short human intro (most visitors arrive with a question about their vision; each service starts with evaluation, diagnosis and a tailored plan; options are explained step by step) and gives each service a two-sentence blurb. No wording was copied.

## Constraints followed

- Existing sections only. No sections added or removed and no layout change.
- No claims: no NABH, ratings, counts, awards, doctors, hours or outcomes. The statement that every treatment is confirmed at consultation stays.
- Blurbs in `site.ts` also appear in the homepage explorer and the related-care lists, so each was kept to one or two sentences and stays general.
- The page stays noindex and `reviewed: false` until sign-off.

## Section-by-section changes

| Section | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Hero lead | One sentence | Adds that whatever brings you, care starts with an examination and a clear explanation of your options (Pixel's "evaluation first, then explained" idea) |
| 01 Comprehensive Vision Care, lead | "Examination, cataract and glaucoma care." | Adds that it is a good place to start for a check-up, a change in vision or a long-term eye condition |
| Cataract Surgery blurb | Assessment and discussion of options | Names the clouded lens; discussion covers when surgery is considered and the options |
| Glaucoma Management blurb | Follow-up needs long-term monitoring | Adds that glaucoma often has no early symptoms and that tests are chosen by the doctor |
| Comprehensive Eye Examinations blurb | Routine check-ups explained in plain language | Says what the check covers: how well you see and how healthy your eyes are, from routine to specific concerns |
| 02 Oculoplasty & Eye-Area Care, lead | One line | Adds comfort, function and appearance, planned after a careful examination |
| Eyelid Surgery blurb | Heavy or drooping lids | Adds under-eye bags and whether surgery would help |
| Aesthetic Oculoplasty blurb | Heavy lids, bags, age-related change | Adds "planned with eye health in mind" |
| Reconstructive Oculoplasty blurb | Treatment for ptosis, malposition, tear drainage | Now "assessment and treatment", and says these problems affect comfort or function |
