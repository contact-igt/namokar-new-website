## Statements on the new pages and their basis
All general medical information was written for this build in neutral terms and **has not been reviewed by a clinician**. Each service page, the doctor page, International, Contact and the homepage are therefore marked `reviewed: false`.

| Page | Statements that need clinician review |
|---|---|
| Cataract | What a cataract is; IOL types in general (distance, near, astigmatism); "stitchless" explained as a small opening that may not need stitches |
| Glaucoma | Optic nerve damage cannot be reversed; managed not cured; risk groups (family history, age, high myopia, long-term steroids, eye injury); normal-pressure glaucoma exists; urgent-symptom note |
| Eye examinations | What an examination may include; drops that widen the pupil blur vision for some hours |
| Eyelid surgery | Blepharoplasty versus ptosis surgery; functional versus cosmetic reasons; general risks list |
| Aesthetic | Assessment-first approach; the page says treatments offered are confirmed at consultation and lists no products |
| Reconstructive | Definitions (ptosis, entropion, ectropion, epiphora, tear-duct blockage, orbit); reasons to be checked |
| International | Says what the centre has NOT promised; steps are a general outline, not stated policy |
