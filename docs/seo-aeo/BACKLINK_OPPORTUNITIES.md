# Ethical backlink opportunities

18 Oct 2026. **No existing backlink was measured (no backlink tool was available or used), none is claimed, no organisation was contacted, and no listing was created.** This is research on where legitimate, earned links could come from, with what each needs first.

Sources used: web searches on 18 Oct 2026, summaries read, pages not individually verified unless stated.

## Rules

- Links come from real relationships, real membership and real usefulness. **No purchased links, link exchanges, private blog networks, comment spam, fake profiles, or paid reviews.**
- Every outreach message needs the clinic's explicit approval, and every listing needs approved facts only (NAP, services). Never claim a membership, accreditation or award that is not proven.
- Doctors' public profiles in India are subject to advertising rules. The legal adviser should clear any listing that carries claims, photographs or ratings.

## E. Not recommended

Buying "Delhi doctor" bulk directory packages; automated submission services; reciprocal link pages; link-farm "guest posts" with exact-match anchors; copying competitors' backlink lists; embedding unapproved ratings; using the doctor's name or photograph on third-party sites without her approval.

## F. To do before any outreach

5. After launch, measure referring domains with a licensed backlink tool or Search Console's links report, and record the baseline here with the date.
