## 1. Executive Summary

**Source and method.** The supplied client outline is `C:\Users\admin\Downloads\website page.docx`. The stated project copy, `client-requirements/website page.docx`, was not present when this audit was performed. Requirements below reproduce the document's wording (with only repeated spaces normalized). The document is a brief outline, not a specification of page copy, button behavior, or acceptance criteria. The assessment is based on the current source, routes, content data, navigation configuration, and asset references; it does not claim a browser or production verification.

The site has a working Homepage, six primary service pages, an About page, a doctor profile route, contact page, and clinic/team photographs within existing pages. The largest gaps against the outline are individual treatment coverage, the requested doctor credentials/media (Barcelona and Johns Hopkins are mentioned, while other named items are absent), a distinct Picture Gallery destination, a populated Blog/Publications/Presentations area, and top-level navigation labels matching the outline. A passing mention, FAQ answer, or redirect is classified as partial or missing rather than a completed treatment page.

**Status key:** **Implemented** = substantive source content or a route exists for the stated requirement; **Partially Implemented** = related content exists but the requested scope is incomplete; **Missing** = no corresponding substantive content or page found; **Needs Verification** = the document is ambiguous, or the source alone cannot establish the intended/public-facing result.

## 7. Recommended Next Steps

1. **Resolve document ambiguities first:** confirm current hero wording, desired top-level navigation, whether Gallery/Team/Doctor need standalone pages, and what “Removal of glasses,” “Cataract Refractive Surgery,” and “Ophthalmic ( Eye ) Surgery” mean in the centre's actual scope.
2. **Collect verified source material:** treatment descriptions for each missing/partial item; doctor training names and evidence; conference photos, memberships, meeting/course records; approved gallery images; DOS/Botox and blepharoplasty articles/presentations, with attribution and publication rights.
3. **Prioritize publication gaps:** build the missing substantive treatment and article content before adding menu links or treating FAQ mentions as complete service offerings.
4. **Confirm operational details:** client-approved contact information, call/appointment workflow, photo consent, medical review, and the source/rights of the pasted blepharoplasty excerpt.
5. **Perform a later browser acceptance pass** after the client supplies and approves content. This audit verified source structure and references; it did not test every rendered route or external action.
