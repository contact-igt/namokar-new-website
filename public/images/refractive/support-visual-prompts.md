# Refractive error supportive visuals

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Final files are `myopia-support.webp`, `hyperopia-support.webp`, `astigmatism-support.webp`, and `presbyopia-support.webp` in this directory. Optimized to 1000px wide WebP quality 84.

Shared prompt: Premium supportive landscape 16:9 website card image, photographic-quality 3D still life with realistic textures, soft warm ivory and peach palette, gentle daylight, elegant medical website aesthetic. No people, labels, diagrams, cross-sections or logos. Keep meaningful objects inside the central 80%.

- Myopia: Distance spectacles on an ivory windowsill, distant tree and buildings clearer through a lens and softly defocused outside it. Emphasize distance focus rather than reading.
- Hyperopia: Open cream notebook and fountain pen on a peach desk; near-work magnifier brings otherwise defocused fine print into focus. No legible words or spectacles.
- Presbyopia: Tortoiseshell reading glasses beside an open fine-print book and warm ceramic cup, ivory linen tabletop, realistic textures, subtle peach backdrop. No people or magnifier.
- Astigmatism (final prompt): Landscape 16:9 image showing only an out-of-focus urban street at dusk through a plain window. Amber streetlights stretched into soft directional streaks and slightly duplicated ghosted lights. Warm peach, ivory and muted teal palette; restrained photographic optical effect. No foreground objects, eyeballs, eye models, bottles, flowers, books, glasses, people, text, medical tools or diagrams. The optical blur itself is the subject.
